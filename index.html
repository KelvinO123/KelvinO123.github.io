Short-bio

Hi, I am Kelvin O'Young, an Electrical Engineering Honors undergraduate at Louisiana State University, with minors in Computer Engineering and Robotics Engineering. I am pursuing a B.S. with an expected graduation in May 2028 and currently hold a 4.02 GPA.

My work spans biomedical instrumentation, embedded electronics, and PCB design. I am a Research Assistant in the Biomedical Engineering Device Lab developing a handheld AI-powered deep-tissue cancer detection probe, and a Powertrain Team member on TigerRacing Formula SAE, where I design safety and monitoring PCBs for a Formula-style electric racing vehicle.

I am passionate about turning circuit ideas into real, manufacturable hardware — from op-amp fault detectors and battery-management protection boards to FPGA-driven state machines. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to collaborate or chat about electronics, robotics, or research.
