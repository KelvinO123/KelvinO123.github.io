BMS Protection Board

TigerRacing Formula SAE · 2025

KiCAD LTspice Battery Management High-Density Layout Manufacturing

Overview

The BMS Protection Board is a compact, fuse-integrated low-voltage board that isolates and safely monitors all 84 cell-level connections coming off the EV's battery pack. It's the protective layer between an accumulator full of energy and the rest of the car's sensitive electronics.

Design Highlights

  • Routed and simulated the protection network to ensure every one of the 84 BMS lines is isolated through its own fuse path before reaching the management IC.
  • Optimized board size aggressively — the harness count keeps growing while the physical envelope inside the car keeps shrinking.
  • Used 1812 metric footprints and consolidated solder points to cut per-board assembly time, which adds up fast across 200+ boards.
  • Validated against an LTspice model of the protection network to confirm correct behavior under expected fault scenarios.

Outcome

The board lets the BMS reliably see each cell while protecting downstream electronics from a stuck cell, shorted tap, or other failure mode. It also dropped manufacturing time considerably, which matters when the team produces them in volume.

What I Learned

Real-world PCB design is a balance between electrical correctness, physical constraints, and manufacturability. Designing for the soldering iron as much as the simulator was a key takeaway.
